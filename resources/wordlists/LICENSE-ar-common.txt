This file is an adaptation of the Arabic OpenSubtitles 2018 frequency list ("ar_full.txt") of FrequencyWords
by Hermit Dave: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
Source content licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
FrequencyWords' lists are built from the OpenSubtitles corpus (http://www.opensubtitles.org/, via OPUS: https://opus.nlpl.eu/).

Changes made: frequencies dropped; only words seen at least 20 times kept; Arabic letters only; each word normalised
(diacritics and tatweel removed; أ إ آ written ا, ة written ه, ى written ي; a leading conjunction «و» and article «ال»
removed from longer words); duplicates removed; sorted, one per line.
